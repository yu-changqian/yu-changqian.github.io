I am a Senior Research Scientist at **Kunlun Tech**, leading the team developing cutting-edge large multimodal models, with a focus on Diffusion and VLM technologies. Previously, I served as a Research Scientist at **Meituan's Autonomous Delivery Department**, gaining deep insights into real-world AI applications.

I earned my Ph.D. degree from the School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, **Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)** in 2021, supervised by [Prof. Nong Sang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ky_ZowEAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) and [Prof. Changxin Gao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4tku-lwAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I was also a visiting Ph.D. student at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning, **University of Adelaide**, working with [Prof. Chunhua Shen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Ljk2BvIAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN).

🏆 During my doctoral studies, I was honored with the **Excellent Doctoral Dissertation Award** (10 recipients nationwide).

My industry experience includes research internships at **Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA)** and **Megvii (Face++)**. I collaborated with [Dr. Jingdong Wang](https://jingdongwang2017.github.io/), [Dr. Gang Yu](https://www.skicyyu.org/), and [Dr. Jian Sun](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ALVSZAYAAAAJ&hl=en). I was also a key member of the winning team for the **COCO & Mapillary Panoptic Segmentation Challenge 2018**.

Research Interests: **Semantic/Panoptic Segmentation**, **Trajectory Prediction**, and **Vision-Language Models**.
