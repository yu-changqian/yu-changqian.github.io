Welcome to my blog! After years of focusing on research and publishing papers, I've decided to start writing more accessible content about AI, computer vision, and my experiences in the industry.

## What to Expect

I plan to write about:

- **Research insights** — Deep dives into interesting papers and emerging trends in computer vision and multimodal AI
- **Engineering lessons** — Practical experiences from building and deploying large-scale models in production
- **Industry reflections** — Thoughts on the evolving AI landscape and where things are heading

## Why Now?

The pace of AI research has accelerated dramatically. While academic papers remain the primary vehicle for sharing technical contributions, there's a growing need for more informal, accessible discussions that bridge the gap between cutting-edge research and practical application.

This blog is my attempt to contribute to that conversation. I hope you'll find it useful.

Stay tuned for more posts coming soon!
